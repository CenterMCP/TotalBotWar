The field manual

You command the Dawn Legion against a bot that also plays with limited vision. Queue one order per regiment. Resolve the turn when ready: both armies move, then attacks land simultaneously. A moving target can evade an attack. Units with no order defend.

RegimentMoveAttack range
Infantry / Spears21

Movement and range use orthogonal tile distance. Water is impassable. Forests block sight; forest occupants hide beyond two tiles. Scouts see farther. Scout orders add two vision for resolution and the next planning phase. Hills and the tower add one vision. Terrain is known from your charts.

Defend reduces damage and restores 8 morale. Forests, hills and the tower reduce incoming damage. Spears counter cavalry. At zero health or 20 morale a regiment breaks and leaves the battle.

Hold I6 for 6 points or eliminate the opposing army to win. After 24 turns the higher tower score wins; equal scores draw.

Try in ChatGPT: “Read my battle and suggest a plan,” “Move Grey Watch toward I6,” or “Queue these orders and resolve the turn.” Keep your battle ID to resume. Anyone with the ID can control the battle.
